JTDX-F modification: FN35's NY-VY entry is corrected to NY-VT.
VY is not a US postal state abbreviation; the locator intersects New York
and Vermont. Lists describe possible states for a grid square, not the
precise location of an individual station.

The loader validates headings, numeric indices and duplicate cells before
publishing the map and never indexes an unchecked fixed-size array.
