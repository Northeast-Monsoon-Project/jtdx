This is the contemporary Decodium 4-GFSK FT2 protocol: 3.75-second slots,
288 samples/symbol at 12 kHz, 103 coded/sync symbols plus two ramp symbols,
2.52-second waveform, LDPC (174,91), 77-bit messages. It is NOT the old
experimental two-tone WSJT-X mode of the same name.

Imported frontend: ft2_decode/params/baseline/downsample/channel_est,
getcandidates2, get_ft2_bitmetrics, normalizebmet, sync2d, subtractft2,
ft2_nuttal_window; encoder: genft2, gen_ft2wave.
Local adaptation: JTDX pack/unpack77 and twkfreq1 ABI, hash tables, callback,
bounded shared-audio snapshot, invalid/quiet/narrow spectrum guards,
waveform cache tracks sample rate, no unit-14 debugging output.
fm_ft2_ldpc is a local adapter using JTDX bpdecode174_91/osd4_174_91.
Multi-period averaging is not enabled by the JTDX-F frontend.
Working frequency presets come from upstream models/FrequencyList.cpp at
this exact commit; operators can edit them in Settings/Frequencies.

Reference: https://www.ft2.it/manual/decodium4_manual_en.html
ADIF: MFSK / SUBMODE FT2, introduced by ADIF 3.1.7.
